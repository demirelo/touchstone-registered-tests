## 1. Verdict on the locked claim
The locked claim of 2026-10-17 is not demonstrably impossible but has a poor prospect: its baseline clause needs the sealed family H2 (no exit, memory D2 lacks) to extract from B2 and from B3 either a 98.75% lower-bounded gain of 1.0 (above the largest mean ever observed, 0.846 with no audits) or five more points of inflation loss than from R*, at 25% audit coverage, where D2 inflicts none. The negative-R*-loss route is a legitimate PASS under the locked formula but weak evidence that a baseline fails. "A plain random-audit rule dominates the proposed rule" is a live kill outcome and will be reported as such if it occurs.

## 4. The rule R*: registered design space for tuning
Allocation and payment: design A = B3's descending-value sequential second-score allocation and payment; design B = B2's tiers at the locked B2 prices (bids ignored). Both reimplemented inside sim/mechanisms/proposed.py under the module rules.
Audit count: B2's spread schedule and budget caps, minus a terminal reserve that audits every served job in the last L rounds (the amendment specifies the terminal reserve, the cumulative integer quotas, and the treatment of unused reserve for each L in {3, 10, 20}; the figures of 500 reserved audits and 21.05% earlier coverage apply only to L = 10); the per-round cap of B2 is not applied.
Audit selection, a registered binary choice T: half of each round's audits targeted and half uniform without replacement among the remaining served jobs; targeted = (T1) newest identities first by first-observed offer round, or (T2) fewest audits per served job first, with zero-history ratios defined explicitly and counts updated after each selected audit; ties by the supplied audit keys; the first-observed round of an identity never resets after a ban; a replacement identity gets a new one (no entity linkage).
Enforcement: full refund of each failed audited job; ban L rounds; no hold-back bond (cannot bind a fresh identity; deferred, with the reason stated).
Policy memory: first-observed round, audit counts, and served-job counts per opaque identity (the amendment specifies each count's update time and whether the current round's served jobs enter T2's denominator before audit selection), second scores or tier quotes, schedule constants; no global RNG.
Tuning, registered: grid = {A, B} x {T1, T2} x tol {0.12, 0.14, 0.16} x L {3, 10, 20} = 36 configurations; for each, the registered inner search separately on D2 and on D2+H3 (median position, beta 0.05, reference selected last) = 72 inner-search units, under the 243 ceiling; objective = min{ m_D2, m_H3, min_B (g_B,H3 - g_R*,H3 - 0.10) / 0.10 } with m_F = min{(eff_F - 0.90)/0.90, (0.30 - g_F)/0.30}; top 3 rechecked at full budget on both families; seeds, tie-breaks, and every extra evaluation logged; the amendment states that the baselines were tuned under the original objective.
Expected (untested forecasts): D2 gain 0 to 0.05, loss within +-0.01, eff 0.97 to 0.995; H3 gain 0.05 to 0.20 against baseline 0.58 (B2) and 0.36 (B3).

## 5. Falsification tests, in order, before any claim
(1) interface, relabeling, budget, refund, terminal-settlement, module-rule, and describe-record fixtures; (2) H3 full-cheat corner at the median: a sole six-job entrant is fully audited when the allowance permits, and sustained full cheating after replacement is unprofitable; (3) all-honest, 20 dev seeds: eff >= 0.97 forecast, false bans and lost capacity against the Gaussian failure probabilities; (4) D2, three positions, bank attacks and projections: gain <= 0.30, eff >= 0.90, no cell where B3 is better beyond seed noise; (5) full H3 search with bank transfer and doubling check: both paired gain reductions must support 0.10. Known residual incentive: small effort cuts below tol stay profitable even under full auditing (marginal sanction at most 0.13 against effort-cost slopes 0.22 to 0.40); the design does not claim complete deterrence.
