# Amendment 1 to the lock of 2026-10-17

Registered before any proposed-rule code or tuning exists. Binding file: `experiments/amendment-2026-10-17.yaml`. Original lock: signed tag `lock-2026-10-17`, commit a5ae297, OpenTimestamps proof in `docs/lock-2026-10-17.txt.ots`. This amendment gets its own signed tag `lock-2026-10-17-amendment-1` and its own proof.

## What the lock said

The claim predicate of spec section 8 at beta 0.05: the rule clause on the sealed family H2 (efficiency at least 0.90, gain at most 0.30, audit within budget) and a baseline clause that needs, for each of B2 and B3, either a gain of at least 1.0 or an inflation-caused efficiency loss at least 0.05 larger than the proposed rule's. The coverage study admitted the studentized bootstrap at 40 seeds only, so H1 is exploratory and H2 is the sole confirmatory family.

## What the registered results showed

At beta 0.05 the audit covers a quarter of served jobs. Against the development attackers, B2 and B3 hold the gain at or below 0.04 and the inflation loss at or below 0.00 (`results/sweep/development.csv`, dev-val means; the figures are in `docs/amendments/roster/brief-proposed-rule.md`). The largest gain seen anywhere is 0.85, by B2 with no audits. The baseline clause can therefore pass only if H2, which does not exit or re-enter, extracts from both baselines five more points of loss than from R*. Where the baselines do break is the public identity-replacement stress test H3: B2 leaks a gain of 0.58 and B3 0.36 with about 180 identities per episode.

## The reviews

Two seats, Sol high (gpt-6.1-sol) and Astra high (gpt-6-astra) through the Codex CLI, answered the same brief independently, were cross-examined on their differences, revised, and voted on a consensus draft; the implementation plan then went through four review rounds until both accepted. Every memo is in `docs/amendments/roster/`. Both seats judged the locked claim's prospect poor and recommended this amendment over changing the budget, recalibrating the thresholds, or admitting H3 into the original clause.

## The change

1. The original predicate is untouched and its verdict is reported in full.
2. A second predicate: on H3 (family `D2+H3`, median position, beta 0.05, 40 fresh final-evaluation seeds), R* must satisfy the same absolute limits, and against each of B2 and B3 the paired per-seed gain reduction `gain(B) - gain(R*)` must have a lower 98.75 percent bound of at least 0.10 (PASS needs both; either upper bound below 0.10 is FAIL; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE). The amended claim is PASS only if the original H2 rule clause is PASS as well.
3. The new statistic gets its own admission: a coverage extension at n = 40 on the six registered cells, with B3 standing in for R* against B2, under the registered gate (6 cells x 2 sides, threshold check, joint fixture, power, the same remedies). It is a proxy admission on development distributions, not nominal coverage for comparisons involving the selected R*, and every report of the amended verdict says so.
4. A binding design-and-tuning registration for the proposed rule (the `design`, `tuning`, and `falsification` blocks of the amendment file), so no material choice stays open after this tag.

## Mechanics

The `claim` block of `experiments/locked.yaml` is inside the settings hash that every tuned file, sweep record, and coverage cell carries, so the amendment never edits that file. It adds `experiments/amendment-2026-10-17.yaml`, the module `sim/amendment.py`, the scripts `scripts/coverage_ext.py` and `scripts/amend.py`, the second predicate in `sim/claimcheck.py` and `scripts/check_claim.py`, and the final H3 cells in `sim/manifest.py`. Those code changes are outside the original lock's allow-list by design: `check_claim.py --verify-only --verify-lock lock-2026-10-17 --proposed none` lists exactly them, and that list is recorded below as the expected delta. From this tag on, the freeze and the evaluation are verified against `lock-2026-10-17-amendment-1`.
