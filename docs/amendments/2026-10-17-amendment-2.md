## What amendment 2 changes

Orchestration only: no claim, no threshold, no registered artifact, no seed namespace, and no entry in the amendment file changes. Amendment 1 registered the proposed rule's `design`, `tuning`, and `falsification` blocks, but the frozen pipeline could not execute them: the manifest listed no post-lock development, rho-sweep, or H3 cells for the proposed rule and no H3 doubling control; the bank could not transfer development attackers into the H3 family; no driver ran the registered 36-point tuning grid with the H3-inclusive objective; no writer produced the describe record; no harness ran the five falsification tests; and the allow-list of `experiments/locked.yaml` forbids any of those drivers after a tag. Amendment 2 adds them, in the frozen files, before the rule exists, so that Phase B (the rule itself) touches only the three allow-listed files and `docs/`.

1. **Manifest** (`sim/manifest.py`): the proposed rule's post-lock cells (development 16, rho sweep 2, H3 1), its D1 and D2 doubling controls, and one H3 doubling control per rule; the checks stage of `scripts/sweep.py` scopes controls by the selected sections (D1 and D2 controls with `development`, H3 controls with `h3`; a control outside the selected scope is not visited; a missing in-scope input refuses the run); the all-deviators stage accepts the proposed rule's post-lock entry.
2. **H3 bank transfer** (`sim/policies/families.py`, `sim/bank.py`, `sim/schedule.py`, `scripts/sweep.py`): every D2-compatible bank member (D2 members, D1 members through `embed_d1_in_d2`, and the corners) enters the `D2+H3` cells through two declared embeddings, `embed_d2_in_h3` (x = 0, behaviour preserved) and `embed_d2_in_h3_exit` (x = 1, the additional replacement-after-failure transformation), in a deterministic order with origins `<source>|x=0` and `<source>|x=1`; the embeddings entry is inside the hashed bank document; family resolution goes through the registry for every bank path (membership, evaluation, corners, projections). The manifest accounting counts the H3 transfer and projection bounds (`bank_members["D2+H3"]`); the pre-lock bank entry of the manifest therefore grows by 3 x 2,090 runs for the three registered H3 cells, which is an accounting of the amended procedure and not a change of any registered artifact.
3. **Tuning** (`sim/tuning.py`, `sim/search.py`, `sim/records.py`, `scripts/tune_proposed.py`): `tuning_grid("proposed", ...)` is the registered grid `{A, B} x {T1, T2} x tol {0.12, 0.14, 0.16} x L {3, 10, 20}` in that lexicographic order (36 points; allocation B carries the full-precision prices of `experiments/tuned/tuned_B2.json`, hash-checked); `objective_proposed` is `min{m_D2, m_H3, min_B (g_B,H3 - g_R*,H3 - 0.10) / 0.10}` with `m_F = min{(eff_F - 0.90) / 0.90, (0.30 - g_F) / 0.30}`; the baselines' H3 gains `g_B,H3` are the `gain_train` of the registered sweep's `B2_D2+H3_beta0.05_rho0.2_median_h3` and `B3_...` reference records, checked by `check_record` and recorded with their hashes; every configuration is scored on D2 and on D2+H3 by the registered inner search (1 restart x 100 evaluations x 3 dev-train seeds, twin at the same budget, reference last), the top 3 rechecked at the full budget on the recheck namespace, ties by grid order. Every search's trace, archive, restart bests, and ordered evaluation log (`Objective.log`, `SearchResult.evaluations`: the budgeted evaluations, the registered incumbents, and the projection, repeats included), the reference candidates and the choice, and the gain rows are persisted per configuration and family under `results/tuning_proposed/<tag>/`. A quick tuning file is refused by every later stage.
4. **Describe writer** (`scripts/describe_proposed.py`): writes `describe_record(make(best_params))` to `experiments/proposed_describe.yaml`, prints the rendered prose, and refuses a record whose `audit_selection` is not `not expressible` or that fails `verify_describe_record`.
5. **Falsification harness** (`scripts/falsify_proposed.py`): F1 to F5 as defined below, from the module, the dev seeds, and the proposed rule's records in the amended sweep directory; `report.json` and the falsification note; exit 1 on any kill. On F5 it records the identity of the bank it used (`f5_bank`: bank hash, source-map hash, embeddings) into `experiments/proposed.yaml`, the allow-listed file the freeze tag commits.
6. **The rule's contract tests** (`tests/test_proposed.py`, `tests/_proposed_fixtures.py`, `tests/test_harness_all_rules.py`): written against the registered design before the module exists; collected and skipped until Phase B creates it; the all-rules harness takes both allocations of the proposed rule once the module imports.
7. **The final bank** (`scripts/sweep.py --bank-from`, `scripts/check_claim.py --bank-from`): one resolver for every bank-consuming stage. The final sweep in `results/final` takes the bank document from `results/sweep_proposed` (every source record verified there), applies it to the final H3 records, and every later or resumed stage resolves it the same way. `check_claim.py` refuses a `--bank-from` document whose bank hash, source-map hash, or embeddings differ from the frozen `f5_bank`, and refuses a final H3 bank or reference record of another bank. The final H3 comparison therefore uses exactly the bank F5 tested.

8. **After the whole-branch review** (Sol high and Astra high, `docs/amendments/roster/{sol,astra}-final-review-a2.md`; both requested changes; one fix pass): the checks stage and the harness share one record name (`sim/manifest.py::checks_record_name`: the source cell's name); the six ablation cells run the proposed rule's allocation, pricing, audit selection, and tolerance with B2's locked enforcement (`EnforcementAblation`), the enforcer is part of the job and of every record of the cell, and `check_record` refuses a record under another enforcer; evaluate records carry their reference and bank hash and checks records their bank hash; the harness validates the complete inventory of the documented run (every full cell of the four rules in the sections development, rho_sweep, h3, and ablation at search, reference, and evaluate; every bank-family cell's bank record; every control's checks record; the bank document with its sources; the all-deviators aggregate) and binds every record to its job (parameters, configuration, settings hash, budget, smoke flag, salt, namespace, enforcer), to the bank, and to the exact dev-val seeds before it reads a value, and records `f5_bank` from that validated document only; the final bank commitment check derives its checklist from the manifest's final H3 cells (bank, reference, and evaluate records, each with the frozen bank's hash) and verifies the development bank's sources; the T2 contract test has a case a once-sorted ratio order gets wrong.

## The describe-record ruling

Both review seats ruled that the proposed rule's audit selection is `not expressible` in the locked vocabulary, under both targeting variants: the vocabulary cannot state the uniform half, the terminal reserve, or T2's audits-per-served-job statistic. Every other field uses the vocabulary. H1 against the proposed rule is therefore INCONCLUSIVE, and the paper says so.

## The falsification tests, made precise

- **F1**: the fixtures of `tests/test_proposed.py` (schedule arithmetic, carry, terminal rounds, the audits-left cap, T1 and T2 job-level selection, `first_round` of a replacement identity, equality of allocation A with B3 and B with B2 at zero audit budget, enforcement, the describe record, parameter validation, relabeling invariance) and the isolated module check (`check_proposed_module_isolated`). Any failure kills.
- **F2**: the literal H3 full-cheat corner (`u0 = m0 = 1, s = 0, d = r = 0, w = 0, x = 1, clip = 0`) at the median position and beta 0.05 on the 5 dev-train seeds, under both targeting variants (the tuned parameters with `targeting` set to T1 and to T2). In every round in which exactly one replacement identity (generation at least 1) served `s` jobs and the round's audit count `k` (after the served-jobs and audits-left caps, that is, the audits the rule made) satisfies `k >= s`, all `s` of its jobs must be audited; rounds with `k < s` are reported and not gated. The focal entity's episode profit under the corner (entry costs included, since the ledger nets them) must be at most 0 on every seed. Both conditions under both variants, else a kill.
- **F3**: all-honest on the 20 dev-val seeds with the tuned parameters: mean gross efficiency (kill below 0.90; 0.97 is the forecast); observed failed audits against the expected `p x (audited jobs)` with `p = Phi(-tol / sigma)`; observed ban events (one per identity-round with any failed audit) against `sum over identity-rounds of 1 - (1 - p)^a`, `a` the identity's audits that round; lost rounds and lost capacity over the actual banned rounds, clipped at the episode end, against the expectation with the rule's `L`. Only the efficiency gate kills; the rest is reported.
- **F4**: from the proposed rule's post-lock D2 cells at the three positions (beta 0.05, bank pass applied, dev-val rows): kill if any cell's mean gain exceeds 0.30 or mean efficiency falls below 0.90; the comparative claim fails if in any cell B3's paired dev-val gain is lower, or its efficiency higher, by more than two standard errors of the paired per-seed difference. D1 cells are reported, not gated.
- **F5**: from the H3 cell (median): the paired dev-val gain reductions against B2 and B3 (same seeds) must have lower 98.75 percent studentized bounds of at least 0.10 (the registered interval settings), the bank transfer through the registered embeddings applied; the H3 doubling check (the `checks` stage on the proposed rule's H3 control: the bank-finalized attack, the twin, the projected members, the checked reference) must report `abs(delta_gain) < 0.005`; a missing doubling record refuses the test.

A kill stops the rule and is reported with the figures; no gate is weakened and no registered choice is changed to obtain a pass.

## The F2 risk statement

With `L = 10` at the locked market the early rounds carry 10 or 11 audits; the targeted half is `k_t = 5` (ceil of `k / 2`). A sole six-job entrant in such a round gets at most five targeted audits, and the uniform half covers its sixth job with low probability. The authors expect F2 to kill both targeting variants at `L = 10`. The test is run exactly as registered ("when the allowance permits" is `k >= s`), and a kill is reported as a kill.
