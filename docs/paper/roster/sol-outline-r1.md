ANSWERS:

(1) The abstract and introduction currently overstate coverage, registration precedence, theoretical scope, and robustness. The recorded original FAIL and amended PASS are supported, subject to the proxy-admission warning. Fresh final seeds must remain distinct from reused development-validation seeds. This is a bounded unilateral benchmark against honest-max opponents, without an equilibrium or complete-search guarantee.

(2) The contribution is plausible as a specific combination, but the broad novelty claim is unproved. [Friedman–Resnick](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1430-9134.2001.00173.x) already establish newcomer dues; [Li](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0022053120300089) combines costly verification and limited punishment. Compare credibility-based verification directly with [Sarmenta](https://groups.csail.mit.edu/cag/bayanihan/papers/ccgrid01/ccgrid01.pdf). Describe static verification models as omitting identity replacement, rather than universally assuming persistent identities.

(3) The plan is broad enough after correction 12. Keep theorem assumptions, negative results, and the strongest matched comparison in the main text. Move detailed hashes, roster history, full grids, and secondary diagnostics to supplements.

(4) Use “Audit Dues: Newcomer-First Verification under Free Identity Replacement.” The current title is long and gives registration more prominence than the mechanism.

(5) AAMAS is a suitable subject fit through [Game Theory and Economic Paradigms](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/dcs/aamas2027/calls/call-for-main-track/). Main-track acceptance still requires a clear mechanism contribution and supported attribution of the measured benefit. The present opening does not meet that standard.
