## 2. Where R3 fails

1. **Audit cost rho = 1 (the allowance equals the terminal reserve).** With alpha = rho m and B = beta S_hat, the allowance is A_tot = beta n_FB / rho = 500 = J L, so the early quota E = A_tot - J L is zero: R3 audits nothing before the last 10 rounds. Its D2 gain is 0.563 (B2 0.087, B3 0.099) and its D2+H3 gain 0.563 (B2 0.792, B3 0.551). The pre-stated prediction ("like beta 0.02, protection weakens") understated this: it is a collapse, and R3 is worse than both baselines under plain adaptive cheating. The design flaw is the reserve rule min(J L, A_tot), which can consume the whole allowance; a reserve capped at a fraction of the allowance would avoid it (not tested; a proposal, not a result).
2. **Noise sigma = 0.10.** With the tolerance fixed at 0.16, honest audits fail with probability Phi(-1.6) = 0.055, bans multiply, and gross efficiency falls for every audited rule (D2: B2 0.635, B3 0.609, R3 0.677; D2+H3: B2 0.520, B3 0.648, R3 0.677). D2 gains are zero for all three audited rules. Under D2+H3, B2 retains gain 0.200; B3 gives -0.008 and R3 gives 0.000. The prediction (efficiency falls most for R3) was wrong in direction: R3 kept the highest efficiency among the audited rules.
3. **Simultaneous entrants (X6, a coalition).** R3's focal gain rises with the coalition size: 0.068 (c 1), 0.118 (c 2), 0.310 (c 5; B3 0.430; B2 0.541 synchronised, 0.543 staggered), inside the predicted ranges (c 2: 0.10 to 0.20; c 5: 0.20 to 0.35). The selected attacks reset after failures (x = 1) and use scheduled resets only every 43 rounds, so the synchronised and staggered profiles' focal mean gains coincide (0.118 and 0.310 under both); other diagnostics differ. At c 5, fresh demand exceeds the round's audits in 178.2 rounds on average and the focal entity's fresh coverage is 0.392, consistent with overload of the priority quota (Lemma 2); these data do not isolate timing as the cause. The matched controls at the selected point are in the evaluation record (`docs/robustness/2026-10-22-evaluate-headline_proposed_X6-SE_c5_synchronised_median.json`, field `controls`): without failure-triggered resets (x = 0) the synchronised schedule gives -0.028 and the staggered one -0.068; with x = 1 both give 0.310.

## 4. Search quality

27 of 30 fixed doubling checks change the dev-train gain by less than 0.005. Three exceed this threshold, all under D2+H3: R3 sigma 0.025 (+0.050995), R3 J60 (+0.017527), and B3 sigma 0.10 (+0.005192). These searches remain incomplete; their reported dev-val means are attack estimates, not certified population lower bounds, and dev-train improvements cannot be added to dev-val means. Nine twin-versus-honest checks fail: B2 at the high position, across all three budgets and the three core families. Restart spreads are in the report per cell.

## 5. What this adds to the paper

Selected attacks show lower R3 gains under identity replacement than under B2 and B3 in many tested variants (budgets, market size and load, horizon, low noise, small entry costs), subject to the stated exceptions and the incomplete searches. At eta 10, the selected audited-rule attacks do not reset; residual gains remain. Corollary 1 concerns free replacement, and the renewal theorem does not cover adaptive D2+H3 or coalitions. Two failures delimit the claim and must be reported: the terminal reserve can consume the whole allowance (rho 1), and coalitions of simultaneous entrants exhaust the priority quota (X6, Lemma 2).
