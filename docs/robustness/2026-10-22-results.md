## 2. Where R3 fails

1. **Audit cost rho = 1 (the allowance equals the terminal reserve).** With alpha = rho m and B = beta S_hat, the allowance is A_tot = beta n_FB / rho = 500 = J L, so the early quota E = A_tot - J L is zero: R3 audits nothing before the last 10 rounds. Its D2 gain is 0.563 (B2 0.087, B3 0.099) and its D2+H3 gain 0.563 (B2 0.792, B3 0.551). The pre-stated prediction ("like beta 0.02, protection weakens") understated this: it is a collapse, and R3 is worse than both baselines under plain adaptive cheating. The design flaw is the reserve rule min(J L, A_tot), which can consume the whole allowance; a reserve capped at a fraction of the allowance would avoid it (not tested; a proposal, not a result).
2. **Noise sigma = 0.10.** With the tolerance fixed at 0.16, honest audits fail with probability Phi(-1.6) = 0.055, bans multiply, and gross efficiency falls for every audited rule (D2: B2 0.635, B3 0.609, R3 0.677; D2+H3: B2 0.520, B3 0.648, R3 0.677). Gains are near zero because shading is detected readily. The prediction (efficiency falls most for R3) was wrong in direction: R3 kept the highest efficiency among the audited rules.
3. **Simultaneous entrants (X6, a coalition).** R3's focal gain rises with the coalition size: 0.068 (c 1), 0.118 (c 2), 0.310 (c 5; B3 0.430, B2 0.543), inside the predicted ranges (c 2: 0.10 to 0.20; c 5: 0.20 to 0.35). The selected attacks reset after failures (x = 1) and use scheduled resets only every 43 rounds, so the synchronised and staggered profiles coincide (0.118 and 0.310 under both). The matched controls at the selected point isolate timing only without failure-triggered resets: synchronised -0.028 against staggered -0.068 (c 5), in the predicted direction but small. The capacity limit of the priority quota (Lemma 2) is real and joint deviation is the way to reach it.

## 4. Search quality

28 of the 30 fixed doubling checks change the dev-train gain by less than 0.005. Two R3 cells moved more: sigma 0.025 D2+H3 (+0.051) and J60 D2+H3 (+0.018); their R3 gains are lower bounds. Twin-versus-honest checks and restart spreads are in the report per cell.

## 5. What this adds to the paper

The newcomer-first rule's advantage under identity replacement is not an artifact of the headline market: it holds across budgets, positions, market size and load, horizon, low noise, and small entry costs, and it disappears exactly where the theory says it should (when replacement is expensive, eta 10, every rule is protected; Corollary 1 explains why uniform rules fail when it is free). Two failures delimit the claim and must be reported: the terminal reserve can consume the whole allowance (rho 1), and coalitions of simultaneous entrants exhaust the priority quota (X6, Lemma 2).
