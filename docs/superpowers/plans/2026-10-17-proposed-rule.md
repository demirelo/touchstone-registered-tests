## Review Focus

- A replacement identity (index at or above the initial N) that offers for the first time mid-episode: `first_round`, `served`, and `audits` start for it then; `capacity` and `banned_until` are indexed by its new index (Task A5's tests, run in Phase B).
- A round with fewer served jobs than the quota, followed by a full round: the carry is spent; the terminal rounds never carry (Task A5).
- Round 0 under T2 with every identity at zero history: ties by `audit_key`, no division (Task A5).
- The sweep's `--rules proposed` on the amended manifest: exactly the proposed rule's development, h3, controls, all_deviators, and ablation cells exist with the names the later stages expect, and `job_for` finds them (Task A1).
- A proposed rule whose `make(params)` lacks `P1` for allocation B, or whose `tol` is outside the grid: `make` raises `ValueError` before any episode runs (Task A5).
