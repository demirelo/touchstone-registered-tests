# Reset-deterrence checks (2026-10-21)

Pre-stated in `experiments/robustness-v1.yaml` (diagnostics) and `docs/theory/reset-deterrence.md` section 8; run with `scripts/check_reset_theory.py` at commit 25bc661 on dev-val seeds 2000 to 2019 (development salt); full output `docs/theory/checks-2026-10-21.json`. Exploratory. Policy: `FixedShade` (claim q_max, bid at truthful production cost, honest while younger than 10 rounds, effort q_max - delta once established, replacement after a failed audit or waiting out the ban). Median provider, T = 200. "Admissible" counts the seeds in which every checked assumption of formula (4) or bound (5) holds (complete-cycle extraction, separate gates; untestable assumptions listed as assumed: independent cycles, stationarity beyond the two-half diagnostic).

## Findings

1. **Corollary 1 (uniform rules, free replacement).** Under B2 and B3 a resetting shader's profit per round barely changes with the shading level (B2 at beta 0.05: 0.381 honest, 0.437, 0.386, 0.376 for delta 0.08, 0.16, 0.24), while a shader who waits out bans loses most of its profit (0.329, 0.101, 0.070). The ban is costless once an identity can be replaced.
2. **Newcomer-first auditing (R3).** At beta 0.05 a resetting shader beyond the tolerance band loses money relative to honest play: 0.178 honest, 0.135 at delta 0.16, 0.090 at delta 0.24. In-band shading (delta 0.08 < tol 0.16) gains a little (0.203), as Proposition 6 predicts. At beta 0.10: 0.179, 0.130, 0.080; in-band 0.198.
3. **Overload (Lemma 2).** At beta 0.02 R3's early quota (2 or 3 audits) is below one entrant's six jobs, fresh coverage is incomplete, and shading pays again (0.176 honest, 0.256 at delta 0.08, 0.189 at 0.16, 0.181 at 0.24).
4. **Lemma 2b.** Under R3 an established replacement identity faces an audit rate of 0.92 (beta 0.05) and 1.00 (beta 0.10) of its served jobs, against 0.17 to 0.21 and 0.43 to 0.48 for initial identities; at beta 0.02 the replacement rate is 0.28. Under B2 and B3 the two rates are equal (uniform selection).
5. **Renewal identity (4).** Admissible only for R3 at beta 0.05 and 0.10 with delta 0.16 or 0.24 (14 to 19 of 20 seeds) and at beta 0.10 with delta 0.08 (5 seeds). There the prediction matches the measured profit per calendar round within 0.002. Because (4) is evaluated with the measured cycle parts and the hazard computed from the distribution of K, this checks the hazard and the cycle accounting; it is not an independent forecast.
6. **Surplus bound (5).** Holds in 63 of the 64 admissible seeds. The exception, R3 at beta 0.10 with in-band delta 0.08 on seed 2011, exceeds the bound by 0.006 (bound 0.027, measured surplus 0.033, standard error over cycles 0.011): within sampling noise of a bound stated in expectation.
7. **Where the theorem does not apply, the gates say so.** B2 and B3 never audit every fresh job (the theorem is about newcomer-first auditing; Corollary 1 covers them); B2's tiered service varies across rounds; R3 at beta 0.02 overloads. Waiting plans are outside the recovery formula by construction.
